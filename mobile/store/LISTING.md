The arena never ends. It just gets harder.

Slide your thumb to dodge bouncers, chasers, lasers, mines and screen-filling bosses while you grab gold orbs to clear each level. Every few levels something new tries to ruin your day, and Petty, your sarcastic commentator, will be there to roast every mistake.

• Endless levels with a boss every 10
• Daily Challenge: one twist, the same run for everyone, its own leaderboard
• Close-call chains: dodge by a hair for RISKY, DAREDEVIL, MADMAN and UNTOUCHABLE bonuses
• Classic, Survival and Boss Rush modes
• Live leaderboards, weekly crown, missions and streaks
• Unlock skins, color packs and upgrades with Moon Coins you earn by playing
• Add Daisy (Southern sweetheart) or Mick (loud Aussie) as your commentator
• Challenge friends with a link and make them beat your score

Built for one thumb. Easy to start, 99% impossible to master.
