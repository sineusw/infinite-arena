Privacy Policy

99% Impossible: Infinite Arena · Petty Games Studio · Last updated November 22, 2026

This policy explains what the game collects and why. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the game.

What we collect

  • Game progress (coins, unlocks, settings, best scores) is saved on your device.
  • Leaderboard entries: if you post a score, we store the name you choose, your score and level, and a random player ID created on your device. We don’t ask for your real name, email, or account.
  • Purchases: in the iOS app, purchases are handled entirely by Apple. On the website, payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card details.
  • Usage stats (website): Vercel Web Analytics counts page visits without cookies and without identifying you.

How we use it

To save your progress, show leaderboards, unlock what you buy, and improve the game. We don’t sell your data.

Names and reporting

Leaderboard names are filtered for offensive words. You can report any name with the ⚑ button; reported names are hidden for you right away and removed after repeated reports.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Delete the app (or clear site data) to remove progress stored on your device. To remove a leaderboard entry, email us with your player name and the board.

Third parties

Apple · Stripe · Vercel
